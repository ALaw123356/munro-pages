Overview

Aonach air Chrith is a 1020 metre Munro located in the Kintail region of Scotland. This route covers approximately 17.8 km with 1970 metres of ascent and is generally considered strenuous by experienced hillwalkers.

Planning this Munro

Before attempting Aonach air Chrith, always check the latest mountain weather forecast, carry suitable navigation equipment, wear appropriate clothing, and ensure you have enough food and water. Conditions in the Scottish mountains can change rapidly.
